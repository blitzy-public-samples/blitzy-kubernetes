## Purpose and Selection Criteria

This document is a reading aid for the code quality audit. The seven catalog documents ([01](01_CONSISTENCY_AND_STYLE.md)-[07](07_TOOLING_AND_PROCESS.md)) define **246 findings** in total, each recorded with the full nine-field structure. That volume is intentional, but it makes the highest-severity items hard to see at a glance. The tables below restate the most significant findings only, in five fields: Finding ID, Title, Category, Source, and a brief Description / Impact.

This document **summarizes existing findings and originates nothing**. It defines no new Finding IDs, no new findings, no new recommendations and no severity judgement of its own; every row paraphrases a record that already exists in documents 01-07. The complete nine-field record for any row — including its Evidence, Inference Flag and Recommendation Ref — lives in the source document named in that row's Source cell, which remains authoritative.

Findings have no severity field, so "most significant" is derived from the audit's own signals rather than from a fresh assessment. A finding appears below if it meets at least one of these criteria:

- **A — P0 source findings.** Cited in the `Source Findings` row of a P0 (Critical) recommendation in [08_IMPROVEMENT_ROADMAP.md](08_IMPROVEMENT_ROADMAP.md) (REC-P0-01 through REC-P0-03). All such findings are included. Matched 16; **16 rows.**
- **B — P1 source findings that the audit foregrounds.** Cited in the `Source Findings` row of a P1 (High) recommendation (REC-P1-01 through REC-P1-07) *and* also surfaced in the Risk Rating Summary or Key Findings Summary of [00_OVERVIEW.md](00_OVERVIEW.md), or in the Risk Register or Architectural Risk Inventory of [09_QUALITY_RISK_ASSESSMENT.md](09_QUALITY_RISK_ASSESSMENT.md). Matched 24; **24 rows.**
- **C — Overview key findings carried into the risk register.** Listed in the Key Findings Summary of [00_OVERVIEW.md](00_OVERVIEW.md) *and* cited by a risk entry (RISK-001 through RISK-006) in the Risk Register of [09_QUALITY_RISK_ASSESSMENT.md](09_QUALITY_RISK_ASSESSMENT.md). Matched 14; **3 rows.**
- **D — Category floor.** Where criteria A-C leave a category with fewer than three entries, that category's Key Findings Summary entries from [00_OVERVIEW.md](00_OVERVIEW.md) are added, so no dimension of the audit is represented by one or two findings alone. Matched 5; **4 rows.**

A finding can satisfy more than one criterion. Each is attributed to the first criterion it meets in the order A, B, C, D, so the "matched" count above is how many findings the criterion selects and the "rows" count is how many appear under that tier after de-duplication.

Rows are ordered by that tier (A, then B, then C, then D) and by Finding ID within a tier. Two Correctness findings that met criterion C (CORR-001 and CORR-005) are not listed, because criterion A alone already contributes 13 Correctness findings and the per-category limit keeps any single category from crowding out the others; both remain fully recorded in [04_CORRECTNESS_AND_EFFICIENCY.md](04_CORRECTNESS_AND_EFFICIENCY.md).
